How RepLog calculates progress

Exercise volume is the sum of weight × reps across completed sets. Workout volume is the sum of its exercise volumes. The progress index compares average load and total reps against the first comparable performance, then combines those ratios with a geometric mean. Declines are shown as they are; they are not judged.

Suggested target weight is for your next workout only. It adjusts conservatively based on time since your last session and whether you met your prescribed reps. It is a suggestion, not a guarantee of strength gains.
